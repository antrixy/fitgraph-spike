Yes. Alpha Progression Pro builds a complete plan around your goal, experience, available equipment, training frequency and preferred workout duration. You can also prioritize specific muscles. Alpha Progression Pro uses your previous performance, the sets you've completed in your current workout, your planned targets and your available weights to recommend a weight and rep count for each working set.
